# Lucy — Product Design Brief

## Status

Design direction is NOT yet approved.

Do not treat the current UI as the target design.

## Audience

Students using Lucy for real college life:
notes, subjects, files, assignments, study and loose information.

Lucy should feel personal rather than institutional.

## Core Job

Help me immediately find, capture or continue what I am working on.

## Core Loop

OPEN
→ recognise what matters
→ continue or capture
→ work with minimal interface interference

## Product Metaphor

Lucy should feel like:

"A really good college desk and notebook, not like software."

This should influence organisation and behaviour without creating a literal desk-themed interface.

## Desired Feeling

Personal
Calm
Capable

With a small amount of youthful personality.

## Lucy Is Not

An LMS.

A university portal.

A project-management application.

An enterprise workspace.

A productivity dashboard.

A Notion clone.

## Design Priorities

1. Student content dominates.
2. Continue working should be extremely easy.
3. Creating a note should be nearly instantaneous.
4. Subjects provide natural organisation.
5. Navigation stays quiet.
6. Writing should remove unnecessary interface chrome.
7. Search should feel powerful without dominating every screen.
8. Empty space is acceptable.
9. Colour should primarily come from identity/content.
10. Lucy should feel owned by the student.

## Current Home Screen

The current screen is reference material, NOT an approved architecture.

Known concerns:

- dashboard-like composition
- dominant permanent sidebar
- two-column grid of repetitive note cards
- workspace statistics
- "Manage" terminology
- repeated Unfiled badges
- excessive containerisation
- weak relationship between product identity and page structure

Do not polish these elements until their need has been validated.

## Current Strengths Worth Exploring

- Lucy name
- Lucy icon
- light overall environment
- clear typography
- legibility
- restrained visual density
- prominent note creation
